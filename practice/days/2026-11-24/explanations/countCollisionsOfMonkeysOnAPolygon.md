# countCollisionsOfMonkeysOnAPolygon

## Intuition & Approach
Binary modular exponentiation computing 2^n - 2 mod (10^9 + 7) representing states with at least one collision.

## Algorithmic Complexity
- **Time Complexity:** $O(N)$ or optimal polynomial bound.
- **Space Complexity:** $O(1)$ auxiliary or minimal recursion/buffer.
