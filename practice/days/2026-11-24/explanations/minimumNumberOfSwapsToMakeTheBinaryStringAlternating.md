# minimumNumberOfSwapsToMakeTheBinaryStringAlternating

## Intuition & Approach
Count parity mismatches against alternating targets starting with 0 or 1, requiring half the mismatches as swaps.

## Algorithmic Complexity
- **Time Complexity:** $O(N)$ or optimal polynomial bound.
- **Space Complexity:** $O(1)$ auxiliary or minimal recursion/buffer.
